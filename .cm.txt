fix(benchmark): report real efficiency scores

`grade()` never receives the session totals, so `grading_result` carries
`efficiency_turns`, `efficiency_tokens` and `efficiency_wall_time_s` at
their 0.0 defaults. Both report generators built their `scores` from that
event, so every generated report claimed the trial was maximally
token- and time-efficient.

`report_scores()` starts from the grading scores and overlays the three
efficiency keys from `trace_end.scores`, which the converter writes from
the session totals. Rubric dimensions still come from the grader, and a
trace without converter scores keeps the previous behaviour.

User-visible changes:
- Generated trial reports show the measured efficiency values instead of
  the grader's zeros.
